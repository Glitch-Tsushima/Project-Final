Historic Places of New Zealand

Explore the locations that hold important stories from Aotearoa's past.

Waitangi Treaty Grounds

Located in Northland, the Waitangi Treaty Grounds is one of New Zealand's most significant historical locations. It is where the Treaty of Waitangi was first signed in 1840 between many Māori chiefs and representatives of the British Crown.

Ōtākou Peninsula

The Ōtākou Peninsula near Dunedin has strong Māori and European history. It was an important area for Ngāi Tahu settlement and later became connected with early European exploration and farming.

Arrowtown

Arrowtown developed during the Otago Gold Rush of the 1860s. The discovery of gold brought thousands of miners from around the world and helped shape the growth of the region.

Hobbiton

Although famous from film, Hobbiton represents New Zealand's modern cultural identity and its connection to international tourism. It shows how New Zealand's landscapes became globally recognised.
